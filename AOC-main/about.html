Attack on Code started as a frustration — finding good teammates for hackathons was harder than building the project itself. So we built the platform we wished existed.

Our Story

In 2024, a group of engineering students sat in a college cafeteria, frustrated. They'd just missed a hackathon deadline because they couldn't find a UI designer in time. Their backend was flawless, their ML model was trained — but no one could make it look presentable.

That moment sparked Attack on Code. We realized that the hardest part of hackathons isn't the code — it's finding the right people, at the right time, with complementary skills.

Today, Attack on Code connects 1,240+ builders across the country. We've facilitated 86 teams, shipped 42 projects, and helped builders participate in 18 hackathons — and we're just getting started.

Our Values

What drives us

Community First

Every feature, every design decision starts with one question: does this help builders connect and build better together?

Radical Transparency

Builder profiles, team progress, project status — everything is visible. Trust is built on openness, not obscurity.

Bias for Action

Ship fast, iterate faster. We'd rather launch something imperfect and improve it than wait for perfection that never comes.

Equal Opportunity

Whether you're a first-year with no hackathon experience or a seasoned builder — everyone deserves a shot at a great team.

Skill over Clout

We celebrate what you can build, not where you study. Contribution history speaks louder than college names.

Relentless Intensity

Inspired by our namesake, we believe in pushing limits. Great things happen when talented people refuse to give up.
